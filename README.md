## Which Walgreens coupon should you try first?

For a general online order, the two most useful current codes are:

- `NOW5`: $5 off eligible items when you spend $25 or more.
- `NOW10`: $10 off eligible items when you spend $40 or more.

The spending threshold is calculated in a single transaction after discounts and before taxes, shipping fees, store credit, and Walgreens Cash redemption. The offer is not truly “sitewide” in the broadest sense because Walgreens excludes several categories, including pharmacy items, gift cards, alcohol, dairy, refrigerated and frozen food, contact lenses, baby formula, selected beauty brands, some toys, pet supplies, and other restricted products.

A useful way to compare the two:

| Order value before tax | Better code | Immediate discount | Approximate discount rate |

| $25 to $39.99 | `NOW5` | $5 | About 12.5% to 20% |
| $40 or more | `NOW10` | $10 | 25% at a $40 order |
| Below $25 | Neither | $0 | No threshold discount |

Do not add random items simply to reach a coupon threshold. If you need $6 of extra products to qualify for $5 off, the order may not actually become cheaper. The exception is when the added item is something you already planned to buy, such as household supplies, personal care products, or eligible Walgreens-brand items.

Both codes are currently listed with an order deadline of **11:59 p.m. Central Time on October 10, 2026**, although Walgreens can change or end an offer before that time. The code must be entered during checkout, and the same product generally cannot receive multiple promotional codes.

## How Walgreens Cash fits into the discount

Walgreens Cash is a reward applied to a future purchase rather than an immediate price reduction. Some promotions award Walgreens Cash after you buy a qualifying product or reach a spending requirement.

For example, Walgreens is currently listing an offer for **$15 in Walgreens Cash when spending $45 or more on selected P&G products**. The order must meet the requirement in one transaction, and the reward is applied after the order is shipped or picked up. The offer also requires an active myWalgreens membership.

This distinction matters when comparing discounts:

- A $10 promo code reduces the current order immediately.
- Walgreens Cash reduces a later eligible purchase.
- A digital manufacturer coupon usually reduces the current item price.
- ShopBack cashback is tracked separately and may take time to confirm.

Walgreens Cash can generally be redeemed in dollar increments on eligible future purchases. Walgreens’ terms state that rewards are applied after tax and after discounts and manufacturer coupons when redeemed. Rewards cannot be exchanged for cash, and exclusions apply to categories such as alcohol, tobacco, gift cards, lottery purchases, shipping, taxes, and certain pharmacy-related items.

Walgreens Cash also has expiration rules. The current myWalgreens terms say active members’ rewards expire on a rolling 12-month basis, while accounts can be considered inactive after six consecutive calendar months without using Walgreens Cash.

That means Walgreens Cash is most useful when you shop at Walgreens regularly. If you rarely place an order, an immediate $5 or $10 coupon is usually easier to value.

## A practical example using `NOW10`

Suppose your cart contains eligible personal-care and household products totaling $46 before tax. You could:

1. Sign in to myWalgreens.
2. Clip any product coupons that match the items.
3. Check whether the clipped coupons reduce the qualifying subtotal below $40.
4. Enter `NOW10`.
5. Confirm that the $10 discount appears.
6. Review shipping and pickup fees.
7. Place the order only after checking the final total.

If a clipped coupon reduces the qualifying subtotal to $38, the cart may no longer meet the `NOW10` threshold. You might then compare two options:

- Keep the coupon and use `NOW5` if the remaining eligible subtotal is at least $25.
- Add a product you already need so the cart remains at $40 or more.

Do not count excluded products toward the threshold. A gift card, pharmacy item, tax, shipping fee, or other excluded purchase may not help you reach the required amount.

## Why a Walgreens coupon may not apply

A coupon can fail even when the product appears to match. Common reasons include:

- The item is a different size or package count.
- The promotion applies to two products and only one was added.
- The cart includes a restricted brand or category.
- The threshold is calculated after another discount.
- The coupon has already been redeemed.
- The offer is account-specific.
- The product is out of stock or back ordered.
- Another promo code is already active.
- The offer expired according to Walgreens’ server time.
- The order is treated as a bulk purchase.
- The item is sold by a third-party partner.

Walgreens’ promotion terms specifically state that offers can exclude clearance products, photo orders, stamps, gift cards, alcohol, dairy, pharmacy items, selected beauty brands, pet supplies, toys, and several health products. Read the offer details rather than relying on the short coupon headline.

## Best strategy for different shoppers

### For a small personal-care order

Look for a product-specific digital coupon first. A $5 off one item can be better than trying to force a $25 order. Use `NOW5` only if you already need enough eligible products to reach the threshold.

### For a $40 or larger household order

Check `NOW10` before using a smaller item coupon. Compare the final checkout totals, because a product-specific coupon may still be better if it can be used without losing the general code.

### For beauty purchases

Search the digital offers by brand and product type. The current `SPOOKYLIP` code is limited to selected Maybelline lip products, while Walgreens’ digital coupon page also shows brand-level deals for products such as Dove, Olay, Colgate, Gillette, and Oral-B.

### For recurring Walgreens shoppers

Walgreens Cash can be worthwhile because rewards can be used on future purchases. Track expiration dates and use the balance before the account becomes inactive.

### For pharmacy purchases

Do not assume a Walgreens coupon or ShopBack cashback applies. ShopBack specifically excludes pharmacy and prescription purchases, while Walgreens offers and reward eligibility can also vary by state and product category.
